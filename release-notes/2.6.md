## Scanned PDFs
- Narrate can now read **scanned PDFs** — pages that are just pictures, with no text you can select or copy. They're read with your Mac's built-in text recognition, entirely on-device, then narrate like any other page (page numbers and footnotes skipped too).
- Scanned pages take a moment to read at first (about a minute for 26 pages); progress shows while it works.
